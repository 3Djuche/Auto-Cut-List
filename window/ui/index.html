When saving a part or switching its configuration in SolidWorks
Gauge

Uses the gauge assigned in SolidWorks, or else the standard gauge that matches the thickness: 0.1196 in steel is 11 GA. Steel (and parts with no material), galvanized and stainless use the Manufacturers' Standard Gauge tables, aluminum uses Brown & Sharpe gauge. Other materials, and thicknesses that match no gauge, keep their measured size.
